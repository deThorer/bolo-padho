Meet Didi — a patient AI reading coach who listens to every child read aloud, in their own language.

Built with Sarvam Voice Agents
The problem

One teacher. Forty children. No time to listen.

A single teacher cannot sit with forty children and hear each one read aloud — so reading practice is the first thing to slip. Children who fall behind in reading fall behind in everything.

Every child deserves to be heard, one sentence at a time.
How it works

Three gentle steps with Didi

No app to install. No reading screen. Just a voice call.

1

Didi says hello

Didi greets the child, learns their name and class, and reads a short story aloud slowly as a model — so the child hears what good reading sounds like.

2

The child reads back

The child reads it back one sentence at a time. Didi catches every skipped, changed or mispronounced word, praises first, then teaches the word syllable by syllable.

3

Stars and a note

Didi asks two simple comprehension questions, gives stars and up to 3 practice words, and leaves a one-line note for the parent or teacher about the child's error pattern.

Features

Kind by design, clever by default

🤗

Never says “wrong”

Didi says “almost!” and moves forward — no scolding, no shame, ever.

🧩

Adapts to the child

Slows to half-sentences when the child struggles, and offers a bonus harder sentence when they read perfectly.

⭐

Star rewards

Every bit of effort earns stars, so children want to come back tomorrow.

🔁

Repeats on request

“Didi, again?” — and the sentence is read once more, patiently.

🌐

Switches language mid-call

Move between Hindi, English and more without restarting the conversation.

🛡️

Child-safety guardrail

If a child says they are hurt or scared, Didi stops gently and flags “needs teacher attention”.

📞

Parent summary

If an adult comes on the line, Didi gives a clear two-sentence summary of the session.

📝

Post-call record

Outcome, practice words and a teacher note are saved after every call.

Why it matters

Reading practice that reaches everyone

Works on any phone

Just a simple voice call — no app, no data-heavy screen, no smartphone required for the coaching.

Scales to a whole class

Every child gets their own turn to read aloud, even when the teacher has forty of them.

Gives teachers data

Clear records show who needs help and with which sounds, so teaching time goes where it counts.
